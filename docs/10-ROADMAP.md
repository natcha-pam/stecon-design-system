# 10 --- Roadmap

## M0 --- Repository & contracts

Solution, projects, docs, AGENTS.md, AI skill, CI-ready build/test
commands.

## M1 --- Foundation + Navigation

Tokens, typography, icons, theme infrastructure, Sidebar variants,
Topbar baseline, AppLayout, Sidebar Gallery.

## M2 --- Core Atoms

Button, IconButton, Input, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Badge, Avatar,
Tooltip, Divider.

## M3 --- Form & Filter Molecules

FormField, SearchBox, FilterItem, StatusBadge, Date wrapper, Pagination,
TableActions.

## M4 --- Enterprise Organisms

FilterBar, DataTable shell, Modal, Drawer, FormSection, DashboardCards.

## M5 --- Templates

ListPage, DetailPage, FormPage, DashboardPage, ApprovalPage, ReportPage,
MasterDataPage.

## M6 --- Adoption

Pilot integration into one application, collect friction, stabilize
APIs, package/version the library, then expand adoption.

Do not start M2+ merely to consume token budget. Complete and review the
current milestone first.
